Delete Your Account

How to delete your Madadgar Kisan account, and what happens to your information when you do.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In the App

Open Madadgar Kisan, go to Profile, tap Delete Account at the bottom, and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out on every phone.

Without the App

Email usmanghanics.dev@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Madadgar Kisan account". We confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What Is Deleted

Your name, email address, mobile number, saved addresses, nearest branch, notification token and preferences, and every sign-in session.

What Is Kept, and Why

Records of orders, payments (including transaction IDs and receipt screenshots) and test bookings are kept for five years because tax law requires it. They are no longer linked to your name or email, and are deleted after that period.
