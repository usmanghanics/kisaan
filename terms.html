Terms and Conditions

The rules for ordering fertiliser and booking tests through Madadgar Kisan, in plain words.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who We Are

Madadgar Kisan is provided by Chaudhary Trading Company, which sells fertiliser from its branches and arranges soil and water testing. BizCare Software Solutions builds and runs the app on our behalf. By creating an account you accept these terms.

Your Account

Keep your password to yourself and your details accurate, especially your mobile number and delivery address. One account is for one person. We may suspend an account that is used to place false orders or to send false payment receipts.

Orders and Prices

Prices are in Pakistani rupees and shown per pack. They can change before you order; the price on your order is the price you pay. Stock is shown per branch, and the branch confirms it when it accepts your order.

Delivery

Delivery is charged by the road distance from the branch to your address. The branch confirms the charge with you by phone before it dispatches, and if it does not suit you, you can cancel at no cost. You can also collect from the branch counter for free.

Payment

You can pay in cash when your order arrives or when you collect it, or by transfer from JazzCash, EasyPaisa or your bank to the account shown at checkout. For a transfer you give us the transaction ID and a screenshot of the receipt, and the branch checks them against its statement before dispatching. The app never asks for card details. A credit account, where the branch has approved one, is settled with the branch.

Cancelling and Returns

You can cancel an order in the app while it is waiting for the branch to confirm it. After that, call the branch. If you paid by transfer and the order is cancelled, the branch returns the payment. Tell us about damaged or wrong goods when they are delivered, or within 48 hours, and the branch will replace them.

Soil and Water Tests

Results depend on the sample given to us. The fertiliser plan in a report is guidance for your crop, not a promise of any particular yield.

Using Products Safely

Follow the directions printed on every bag. We are not responsible for crop loss that comes from using a product differently from those directions.

Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms. When we do, the app will tell you before the new version applies.

Contact

Questions about these terms: usmanghanics.dev@gmail.com.
