Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we need it, who sees it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your information.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

What We Collect

Your name, email address, mobile number, nearest branch and delivery addresses. The orders you place, and for a transfer, the transaction ID and the receipt screenshot you attach. The tests you book, with what you tell us about the field (crop and size) and the results. A notification token for your phone, so that order and test updates reach it.

Why We Need It

To create and protect your account, take and deliver your orders, check transfer payments, arrange your soil and water tests, keep you updated on orders and tests, and improve the service our branches give you.

Photos and Location

The app opens your camera or gallery only when you choose to attach a payment receipt, and sends only the picture you pick. It asks for your location only when you choose to use it for a delivery address; you can always type the address instead.

Who Sees It

Our branches, the courier company that delivers your parcel (your name, phone number and address, so it can reach you), and the laboratory that tests your sample. BizCare Software Solutions hosts the app and its data. Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers notifications to your phone, our email provider carries the sign-in codes we email you, and Google Maps finds the address you search for and measures the road distance for the delivery charge. Nobody else, and we never sell it.

How It Is Kept Safe

Your information is stored on servers operated by BizCare and travels over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored only in a form that cannot be read back. Receipt screenshots are never on a public web page: only you and branch staff can open them.

How Long We Keep It

Order, payment and test records, including receipt screenshots, are kept for five years because tax law requires it. Your profile, addresses, notification token and preferences are removed when you delete your account.

Your Choices

From Profile you can correct your details, turn notifications off, and delete your account at any time. You can also ask us to delete your account without the app; see the account deletion page.

Children

Madadgar Kisan is meant for adults buying for a farm or a business. It is not directed at children.

Contact

Questions or requests about your information: usmanghanics.dev@gmail.com.
